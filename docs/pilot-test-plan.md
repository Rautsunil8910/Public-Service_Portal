# Pilot test plan

**Purpose:** confirm the portal does what clerks, supervisors and residents need (functional compliance) and that they find it easy to use (satisfaction) before wider rollout. (JD duty 7.)

## Participants

Recruit 3–5 people (friends or classmates are fine for a portfolio pilot): two act as clerks, one as a supervisor, one or two as residents.

## Tasks

| # | Role | Task | Success criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Resident | Create an account and report a pothole outside your house | Done in under 3 minutes without help; receives request number and target date |
| 2 | Resident | Find out the status of your request | Finds it from *My requests* without help |
| 3 | Clerk | Take a phone report of a streetlight out (caller gives name + email) | Correct category, municipality and caller recorded |
| 4 | Clerk | Triage all new requests | Each moved to Triaged; urgent ones prioritised |
| 5 | Supervisor | Assign the pothole to a crew, record a $350 cost, close it | Status Closed; cost shows; later shows "Posted to Finance" |
| 6 | Supervisor | Call the resident back about task 5 | Reveals contact with a reason; reason appears in the privacy audit |
| 7 | Resident | Get a fee estimate for a $40,000 deck, then apply | Estimate matches the submitted fee |
| 8 | Supervisor | Export SLA compliance to Excel | File opens with the expected months |

Record for each task: completed (Y/N), time taken, number of help requests, and errors made.

## Survey (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)

1. It was easy to find what I needed.
2. The words on the screens made sense to me.
3. I always knew what would happen next with a request.
4. I would be comfortable using this every day.
5. What was the most confusing moment? *(free text)*
6. What one change would help most? *(free text)*
