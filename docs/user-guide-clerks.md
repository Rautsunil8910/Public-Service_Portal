# Service Portal – Quick guide for Customer Service Clerks

This guide covers the three things you'll do most: take a report by phone, triage new requests, and answer "where is my request?" calls.

## Signing in

Go to the portal and sign in with the user name and password IT gave you. After five wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. If that happens, wait, or call the IT service desk to unlock it.

## 1. Taking a report by phone or at the counter

1. Click **Report a problem**.
2. Under **Caller**, choose how it came in (Phone, Counter or Email).
3. Enter the caller's name and, if they have one, their email. *If they've called before, using the same email links the request to their existing record.* For an anonymous tip, leave the caller fields blank.
4. Choose the **category**. The screen shows the service standard, for example "Service standard: 5 business days". Tell the caller this.
5. Choose the **municipality** and type the **location** as precisely as you can: house number, road, nearest intersection, or which lane.
6. Describe the problem in the caller's words.
7. Leave **Priority** on *Category default* unless there is a safety hazard (for example, a signal out at a busy intersection). Then choose *1 – Urgent*.
8. Click **Submit request**. Read the **request number** (SR-…) and target date back to the caller.

## 2. Triaging new requests

1. Open **Request queue**. Overdue requests are always at the top, then by priority.
2. Filter **Status: Submitted** to see what's new.
3. Open a request and check that the category and location make sense.
4. Under **Next step**, choose **Triaged**, adjust the priority if needed, add a short internal note, and click **Update request**. The department supervisor now sees it and will assign a crew.
5. If it's a duplicate of another request, choose **Cancelled** and put the other request number in the note.

## 3. "Where is my request?"

1. In **Request queue**, type the request number (SR-0001234) or part of the street name into **Search**, and set Status to **All**.
2. The **Timeline** shows every step and the date it happened. The **Target date** shows when it's due.
3. If the request is **Overdue**, tell the caller you'll flag it, and add a note so the supervisor sees it.

## Why are phone numbers and emails partly hidden?

Residents' contact details are protected under the *Municipal Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act*. You'll see them masked, like `XXX-XXX-0142`. Supervisors can view them when there is a work reason, and each view is recorded. If you need to call someone back, ask your supervisor.

## If something goes wrong

If you see **"Something went wrong"**, the screen shows a reference code. Copy it into your service desk ticket. IT can use it to find exactly what happened.

If you see **"This request was changed by someone else"**, a colleague updated the same request while you had it open. Reload the page, check their change, and try again. Nothing was lost.
